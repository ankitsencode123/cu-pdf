# Calcutta University — CS Study Archive

Public file repository backing **The Stacks** frontend archive for Calcutta University Computer Science (**B.Sc**, **M.Sc**, and **M.Tech**).

## Contents

- `Bsc/` — B.Sc Computer Science previous-year question papers by subject
- `Msc/` — M.Sc Computer Science (Semesters 1–4): textbooks, notes, assignments, lab code, syllabi, and PYQs
- `Mtech/` & `M.Tech/` — M.Tech CSE (Semesters 1–3): textbooks, notes, syllabi, and PYQs
- `aa samim da/` — Advanced Algorithms textbooks, lecture notes, and assignments
- `RESEARCH METHODOLOGY/` — Research Methodology reference book and lectures

## Attribution

Compiled for student study use. Base collection includes materials originally curated in `zubrist/Calcutta_University` and `subhrapratim07/M.Tech`, alongside additional semester notes and question papers. All textbooks remain the property of their respective authors and publishers.
